## Why a wiki?
I've collected many reference points over the years, from pop culture to history to academic concepts and best practices. I also tend to give out advice on similar subjects, and frankly waste time rewriting the same explanations.

Books and blogs are great formats to build an argument or narrative.  But my aim is to collect and organize ideas on an open-ended basis, which is well matched to a wiki structure.
