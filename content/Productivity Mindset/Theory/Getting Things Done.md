Getting Things Done by David Allen

In my initial read, this book was very alienating. The self-surveillance required in optimization tends to be stressful and demoralizing to me. There's a lot of business buzzwords that don't really apply to my situation. Honestly, even though I self-assigned it, the fact that it became a school assignment made it feel like less of a choice. 

I plan to revisit this in future, given some distance from this assignment.
