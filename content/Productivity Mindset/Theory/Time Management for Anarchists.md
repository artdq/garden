Comic - Time Management for Anarchists by Jim Munroe and Marc Ngui

Reading this comic was motivating! It is possible to both be critical of hierarchical work modes and get things done. This was an important reframing for me in connecting work practices to my political values.

The core thrust relates to a minor point in *Saving Time* — where we may label those who seek to climb the corporate ladder as "ambitious", for some reason we don't associate that trait with people who pursue community organizing, even when the outcomes they seek (societal change) are clearly more "ambitious."
