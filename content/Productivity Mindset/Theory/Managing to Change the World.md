Managing to Change the World by Alison Green and Jerry Hauser

I revisited the sections on prioritization and time management. The advice has previously been useful to me in an office setting, especially around managing up and delegating tasks. 

One drawback to this text WRT the school environment is that the software, shared organization systems, schedule, and key collaborators are much more temporary than an office setting. 

The most enduring takeaway might be the tips around efficiently communicating about what needs to get done. The communication tips around managing up like how to structure an email to get the information and decisions you need, were useful in effectively communicating with my working groups.
