Saving Time by Jenny Odell, 2023

Highlights:
- The popular concept of productivity treats time working as fungible: any hour or minute able to be exchanged with any other. There is a moral valence to productivity as virtuous. 
- Taylorism - the roots of industrial optimization culture
- Achievement subject vs obedience subject - motivated by the can not should
- Sense of safety underlying leisure. 
- Bearing the weight of grief in climate catastrophe

This book is a bit fuzzy and conceptual, which makes it a breath of fresh air in thinking about work styles. To repress the politics and injustice and philosophy embedded in how work is experienced in my life — that just won't speak to me.
