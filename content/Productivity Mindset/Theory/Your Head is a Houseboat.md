Your Head is a Houseboat by Campbell Walker

This book helped me feel more grounded and in control. The core thrust is about reaching mental clarity through journaling practices. It uses different metaphors to reframe thought patterns. The most resonant chapter for me, re: this goal and this stage in my life, was about envisioning your future and having specific goals to plan toward. 

This paired really well with the idea of "seizing the time" in *Saving Time*. My future is more unwritten than ever right now. A lot of my decision making around what comes next has been oriented around what *seems like* the smart or strategic decision, but it's been leaving me unmoored and unmotivated. Reframing toward things I specifically want has helped me find a bit more direction amidst uncertainty.
