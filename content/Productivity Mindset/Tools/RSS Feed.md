The habit of repeatedly checking and scrolling news sites on my phone was causing me anxiety. I started using an RSS feed, Newsblur, to follow news sites, newsletters, and blogs that I find essential. 

An RSS feed is an aggregates updates across websites you subscribe to. There is no recommendation algorithm and no infinite scroll. This removes the "slot machine" effect of using a feed.

Reading through my RSS feed can still be a time sink, however I find myself more consciously engaged and less anxious (and therefore more able to self moderate). It also moves the task from my phone to my laptop, so I check it less frequently.

Use case: mediating a healthier relationship with media consumption
Rating: A. This has been vital to balancing my mental health and energy.
