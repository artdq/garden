# About
In this version, I started writing out a site specifically for publication, limiting its use case to a digital garden. I drafted a home page, planned a few topic areas, and drafted a page about graphic design. 

The purpose of this version was to home resources I frequently use and create reusable documentation for peer-to-peer and mentorship collaborations. I tend to give similar advice repeatedly (f.e. graphic design basics), and can spend an inordinate amount of time doing so. It's one of those things that I enjoy a lot, so it can balloon in my schedule a bit. By having reference documents, I can be a bit more time efficient.

# Reflection
This is not a complete, ready-to-publish version. While it has the potential to be a timesaver, it's also a bit of a vanity project. I set it aside as it's not super related to my studies at the moment.
