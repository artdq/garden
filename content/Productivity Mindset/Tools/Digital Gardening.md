Digital gardening is an organization system kind of like a wiki. It uses an open ended, branching structure to organize information and ideas. 

Use case: documenting information sources, project notes

Rating: C. I've switched to a wiki-type software called Obsidian as my main word processor for scratch documents, and to organize project notes (f.e. on the final project) when not handwritten. I like the idea of it, but I haven't figured out an organization system where I consistently file my new entries and find what I'm looking for in my old ones. Part of this could also be that I don't have stability in the types of work I'm doing.
