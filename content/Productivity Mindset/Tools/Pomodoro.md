Pomodoro is a time management technique breaking work into intervals with breaks. 

I used two formats:
- Focus Friend app, a "gamified" version
- setting a phone timer

This has been a strong time management technique when I am close to a deadline.

Use Case: Adding urgency to tasks that could take much longer
Rating: B. It works well, but I tend to reach for it as a worst case scenario.
