Tom Nicolas - Why YouTubers Hold Microphones Now (2023)
Essay about internet video aesthetics, the function they serve in media

- Holding a lav mic is "gleeful unprofessionalism" (Note, increasingly misusing greenscreens have been adopted, e.g. David Achu floor greenscreen; meta-commentary within the video e.g. Ash Bentley; the miced-up off-screen producer e.g. Hivemind)
- He uses Prager U as the antithesis of YouTube native - corporate video aesthetic, invisible mics, polished, clipart, logo in the corner
- Aesthetics generally work in social and cultural taste - in-group signaling and audience acceptance
- Internet Ugly aesthetic, coined by Nick Douglas, 2014
- Case studies:
- Corporate channels like Good work,  politics joe -- corporate projects but with a culturally native voice
- Vox-owned channels like Polygon - "resident creator" model, in which presenters are shown like auteurs, independent vision and unique creative voice 
- Values of amateurism and participation are represented by rejecting professional aesthetics, however, notably the held microphone produces quality audio. (Note, sets may be cluttered but never unclean)
- Tension between professionalism and participatory DIY
