# About
Hank Green is a titan of YouTube culture -- alongside his brother John Green, he's been a creator on the platform for decades and has had a role in shaping institutions like Vidcon and Crash Course. I find he is often very insightful about the platform, creator economy, fan communities, and science communication. 

I'm interested in content creation that is informative and entertaining, and he is a creator I admire in this regard.

# Highlights
- Rooting the format in the brothers' relationship and personal narratives grounds the often conceptual discussions. The audience cares because the Green brothers care. The frequent posting supports this relationship-oriented.
- His video formats themselves are generally conversational, like his presentation style. Some formats build from audience engagement (responses/questions submitted on socials).  Some are directly in dialog with other creators (interviews, stitches). Even the research-a-long feels dialogical, as he commentates on research sources.  It's all built around thoughtful engagement with a person/topic. While he's generally placed in an educator role, it's not a one-way lecture.
- As an interactive medium, content formats based in conversation can cultivate an audience thoughtfully engaging in the subject matter.
# Notes

Observations on his video content family, how he engages audiences

Vlogbrothers (alongside John Green)
- 5-10 min, posted weekly
- Generally responding to the others' last video, current events, or something going on in their lives
- Often musing on abstract/thematic subject matter, but starts with personal anecdote -- How did I arrive at this opinion/insight. The personal narrative element is core to the vlog genre, but also works as a hook (via narrative transportation theory)
- Always framed as correspondence to the other brother (as part of the original concept of the channel)
Analysis: Rooting the format in the brothers' relationship and personal narratives grounds the often conceptual discussions. The audience cares because the Green brothers care. The frequent posting supports this relationship-oriented.

HanksChannel (his personal channel, mostly around science communication)
- tiktok/shorts
	- stitches - explaining a science concept
		- responding to misinformation, expanding on curiosity
		- sometimes swerves to a joke, instead of a thoughtful, scientific response
	- responding to questions/requests
	- clips reformatted from longer videos
- long form
	- research-a-long - he's interested in a question and walks through how he finds an answer, often filtering through scientific papers
	- loosely structured topic discussions - he often doesn't outright announce the purpose of the video until its well underway. Often about science news, or something new he's learned about.
	- posing a question to his audience on bsky/YT post, going quickly through the answers and responding to them
	- interviews
		- starts with him setting up the topic, expanding with informational context then the interview, often with an author or public figure
		- Hank as an interviewer is very conversational -- he finds commonalities with his own knowledge and the subject
		- Usually announced to be an interview until segueing into the interview 
	Often starting with daily context, why he's making the video (like the vlogbrothers format)
Analysis: His video formats themselves are generally conversational, like his presentation style. Some formats build from audience engagement (responses/questions submitted on socials).  Some are directly in dialog with other creators (interviews, stitches). Even the research-a-long feels dialogical, as he commentates on research sources.  It's all built around thoughtful engagement with a person/topic. While he's generally placed in an educator role, it's not a one-way lecture.

As an interactive medium, content formats based in conversation can cultivate an audience thoughtfully engaging in the subject matter.
