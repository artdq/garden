# About
This video is an interview with the CEO of Dropout, an independent streaming service for comedy content. They produce primarily unscripted comedy series including game shows and DnD letsplays with a rotating cast of comedians. They are known for high-concept props and costuming, as well as an inclusive atmosphere with a vaguely anti-capitalist bent.

How Dropout Beat Big Media by Being Weird - The Atlantic with Sam Reich
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3XscYNAA2sk
# Reflection
I find Dropout a very interesting media organization. It grew out of a sketch comedy channel, College Humor, and now operates as a streaming service with a dedicated fanbase. As a consumer, I don't identify with many larger professionalized video producers, but I align with the Dropout brand. This interview was a lightbulb moment for me in how the CEO describes their position in the industry. The label of "alt-media" I think encompasses the type of brands I'd like to work with.

# Notes
Theme: alt media operates with a different business model and audience relationship -- this is (in part) how the Dropout model works

- Contrarian personality; indie media mindset; rebellious spirit
- Part of the production criteria is that it needs to work in both longform and shortform video, so it markets itself
- Performer Personality-driven -- part of what makes new comedy hard to sell is audience trust in a new comedian -- a rotating cast solves that problem.
