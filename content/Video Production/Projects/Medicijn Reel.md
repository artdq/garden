# About
For my second video project, I worked with a client on a vertical video. It focused on the post-production phase.

I worked with my coach Kevin Brouwer to edit a reel for a client, promoting their upcoming theatre production Medicijn. I was provided with footage of members of the show answering the same questions. How they may be edited together was up to me.

Since the footage is in Dutch, I first had to translate it. Using Premiere Pro's automated transcriptions with Microsoft Word's translation tools, I was able to get a mostly accurate transcript to use while editing.

# Feedback
I requested the following feedback from Kevin:

> I wanted the clips to respond to each other like a conversation with forward momentum. Which was maybe an ambitious choice for my current language level 😅 In particular, I broke up sentences to keep the pace moving. After the intro the “als ik een pill zou zijn…” is left implied and some sentences/ideas are broken into two clips. This is a style I’ve seen work in English media, but I don’t know if I pulled it off in Dutch. I’d love feedback on whether it feels natural in terms of the sequence and pacing, as well as the dialogue selection.  
> 
> Tone-wise, I tried to keep it candid and lighthearted. When I included laughs/silly faces/etc, I wanted it to be endearing, not awkward or unflattering to the subjects. Did I achieve that?  
> 
> In the color correction and noise reduction I aimed to reduce distractions without being too noticeable. I wouldn’t say it’s cinema quality but I think I got it to be unobtrusive. Is there anything you would change about these elements?

I received the feedback:
- Tone and pacing generally work except two parts
	- The pacing is could be tightened up in the first third
	- The client may be iffy on the drug references, and those might need to be cut. Alternatively, the can be place next to each other and sold as a joke more by adding some psychedelic filter effects.
- A few areas for improvement in the audio 
	- The transition between clips is a bit clunky. Fading the audio in and out for each clip will soften it. 
	- Background music or sound effects could add energy. Music from the show can be downloaded from the YouTube channel, or you can easily make record sound effects yourself.
- It makes sense in Dutch except for one part -- there is a "hi" at the start of one clip that feels out of place
- Color correction is satisfactory
