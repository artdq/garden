# About 
For my fourth video project, I went to Keukenhof and filmed footage for a montage reel. It focused on the production and post-production phases.
# Production
Filming Date: 3 April, 2026

Goal: take vertical video footage for a short-form POV montage capturing the atmosphere of a visit to the Keukenhof

Shooting handheld with the main (rear) camera of my iPhone 13 mini
No audio capture beyond built-in
Bring new powerbank 

Coverage wanted:
- variety of types of shots - particularly medium and wide; still and moving 
- aesthetically pleasing framing
- capture the sights - windmill, gardens, greenhouse
- include crowds in the background, but avoid identifiable footage of strangers

# Post-production
Part 1: screening and organizing footage
![[Screenshot 2026-09-13 081244.png]]
Screenshot of footage organization

Workflow:
- All assets from the day imported into Premiere pro project
- Assets organized into bins by type - vertical vs horizontal, video vs live photos
- Vertical video screened and labeled. 
	- Given descriptive keyword labels
	- in and out points set on all clips
	- longer clips divided into subclips where there are multiple usable segments
	- comments rate the quality in 0-3 stars
	- 2 and 3 star footage copied to a select bin
- Select clips placed on a timeline in chronological order and exported

Part 2: editing into a rough cut for social
Workflow:
- Background music selected - Ramones cover of what a wonderful world
	- Rationale: thematically connected lyrics, energetic rhythm for editing, a positive but slightly bittersweet feeling to mirror the grey sky and winterwear 
- markers set (roughly) in time to music to give the edit structure
- clips cut down even further. narrowed down and rearranged for variety, movement, and rhythm.
